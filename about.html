About MaxLase

Why we built a US support layer around an overseas-built machine.

Most laser welders sold in the US are manufactured overseas — that's not a secret, and it's not the problem. The problem is what happens after the sale: no local parts, no real training, no one to call when a lamp burns out. MaxLase exists to fix that.

Our approach

Made overseas? Yes.

At that price-to-quality ratio, it can't be beat — which is exactly how MaxLase offers the most machine for the money. What you don't get from a straight import is US support. That's the gap MaxLase exists to close: we stock parts, install on-site, train your team, and repair or replace your system in person if it comes to that — because when your application can't afford to be down, we make sure you're not the one waiting on it.
