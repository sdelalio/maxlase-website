About MaxLase

Why we built a US support layer around an overseas-built machine.

Most laser welders sold in the US are manufactured overseas — that's not a secret, and it's not the problem. The problem is what happens after the sale: no local parts, no real training, no one to call when a lamp burns out. MaxLase exists to fix that.

Our approach

Manufacturing quality was never the issue.

MaxLase systems are built by an established overseas manufacturer with a strong track record in pulsed Nd:YAG laser welding equipment. The build quality is good — that's not what stops independent jewelers and small manufacturers from buying one. What stops them is buying a $10,000+ piece of equipment from a company with no US presence, no local parts, and no one to train their team.

Weld Solutions built MaxLase to close that gap: every system is inspected and configured before it ships, backed by US-based service and spare parts, and supported by real training — on-site or virtual — for your actual application.
