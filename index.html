Why MaxLase

The machine isn't the whole product.

MaxLase welders are manufactured overseas by a proven partner — the same way most precision equipment is built today. What makes MaxLase different is everything around the machine: US-based configuration, service, spare parts, training, and engineering support, offered exclusively through Weld Solutions.

1

Inspected & configured stateside

Every system is checked, calibrated, and set up before it ships to you — not dropped at your door straight from a container.

2

Real service, real parts

Flashlamps, transformers, and wear parts stocked domestically. No multi-week waits on overseas freight for a $60 lamp.

3

Training that sticks

On-site or virtual training on your actual application — ring repair, prong retipping, tab welding, sensor housings, and more.

Full-power, benchtop-sized

Portable benchtop convenience. Full-scale welding power.

MaxLase fits on the same bench as your microscope and hand tools — no dedicated room, no 3-phase power, no oversized footprint. But the compact size doesn't mean compromise: every system runs the same Nd:YAG welding core as machines twice its size, so you get full penetration and full duty cycle in a unit that pulls up to your existing workstation.
