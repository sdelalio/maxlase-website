Overview

The most popular system in the lineup

The MaxLase 200 is the system most jewelers and small manufacturers land on — enough pulse energy and spot-size range to handle everything from delicate filigree to small industrial components like sensor housings and battery tabs, with weld penetration up to 2mm on suitable materials.

  • Compact bench-top footprint, 220V single-phase
  • Built-in water chiller, no external plumbing
  • Compatible with D65/D69 tilt-rotary and manual X-Y table
