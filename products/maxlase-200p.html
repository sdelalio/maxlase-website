Overview

Built for production, not just the bench

The MaxLase 200P moves the same 200W Nd:YAG platform into a floor-standing pedestal with a built-in chiller and a motorized work table — for shops running higher volume, tighter tolerances, or wanting a dedicated production station separate from a bench-top unit.

  • Floor-standing pedestal, built-in water chiller
  • Largest rotary compatibility in the lineup: D69 and D80
  • Motorized, ballscrew-driven work table (not a manual X-Y table)
