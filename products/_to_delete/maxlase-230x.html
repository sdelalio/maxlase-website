Overview

The most power in a bench-top footprint

The MaxLase 230X tops out the bench-top lineup with the most pulse energy and highest input power in the range — for shops that need extra margin on thicker material or higher-volume repair work, without moving up to a pedestal system.

  • Largest bench-top footprint in the lineup, 220V single-phase
  • Built-in water chiller, no external plumbing
  • Compatible with D69 tilt-rotary and manual X-Y table
Cool Features

The most advanced control interface in the lineup

The 230X is more than the highest-power bench-top system — its articulating touchscreen brings smart-device convenience to the bench.

  • Voice control — hands-free adjustment of weld parameters while you're mid-repair.
  • WiFi & Bluetooth connectivity — pair accessories and pull firmware/parameter updates without a cable.
  • Built-in AI assist — smart parameter suggestions to help dial in new materials faster.
  • Multicolor ambient lighting — an RGB light bar around the work chamber for status at a glance and a bit of shop flair.
  • MP3 / MP4 multimedia player — play music or reference video right on the machine while you work.
  • High-definition articulating touchscreen — swings and tilts to the angle that's comfortable for you.
  • Cloud-ready control software — the same easy-to-learn interface as the rest of the MaxLase line, with more horsepower under the hood.
