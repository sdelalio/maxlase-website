Why jewelers switch to laser

Cleaner repairs, less rework, happier customers.

◆

No flux, no fire scale

Weld directly next to stones and delicate settings without heat damage or discoloration from soldering.

◆

Stone-in-place repair

Retip prongs and repair settings with diamonds and gemstones still mounted — no more de-setting for routine repairs.

◆

Precise, repeatable spots

Spot sizes down to 0.2mm give you control that a torch simply can't match on fine filigree and thin-gauge findings.
