MaxLase 1500H / 2000H / 2500H

Air-cooled handheld fiber laser welders. No chiller, no water loop, no 3-phase service — plug into 220V single phase and weld. A 600 g gun, wire feed, and adjustable wobble put production-speed welds on stainless, steel, galvanized sheet, brass and aluminum.

Ideal for

Fabrication, sheet metal, and repair work off the bench

Where the benchtop and pedestal systems are built for jewelry and small precision parts, the H-series goes to the work: railings, enclosures, tanks, frames, kitchen and food-service equipment, signage, and general fab shop repair. Welding runs 6 to 10 times faster than TIG, and the seam usually needs little or no cleanup.

  • ✓ Air cooled — dual-circuit refrigerant direct cooling, no external chiller to plumb or maintain
  • ✓ 220V single phase, 50/60 Hz — no 3-phase service required
  • ✓ 600 g welding gun on an integrated armored cable
  • ✓ Laser power, wobble width and wobble frequency adjust right on the gun
  • ✓ Built-in wire feeder for 0.8 to 1.6 mm filler wire — needed once the joint gap is over 0.2 mm
  • ✓ Rated to run continuously, and to work from −20°C to 60°C
  • ✓ Most operators are welding within about 30 minutes of training
