Why small, powerful Nd:YAG

Right-sized power for small, precise parts.

●

Low heat-affected zone

Pulsed Nd:YAG keeps heat input tightly controlled — critical for thin gauges, dissimilar metals, and components near sensitive electronics.

●

Penetration up to 2mm

Enough energy for structural tack and seam welds on small parts, without the cost or footprint of a kW-class system.

●

Repeatable, programmable settings

Dial in pulse width, frequency, and current by material — then repeat it exactly, run after run.
