Tips from the bench

Jewelry & tacking tips

Short, practical notes — this list grows as we publish more. Have a tip or technique worth sharing? Send it our way.

Always test on scrap first

Metal purity, plating, and prior heat history all shift what "correct" settings look like — a 30-second test weld on scrap saves a ruined piece.

Clean the joint before you weld

Oils, polish residue, and oxidation cause inconsistent welds and pitting. A quick ultrasonic clean or alcohol wipe before welding makes a visible difference.

Lower frequency for stone-in-place repairs

When welding near a mounted stone, dropping frequency and pulse width reduces heat buildup — keep an eye on total pulse count during longer repairs.
